Simulating a flock from three simple rules

personal · python, pygame, javascript · written without AI

OBJECTIVE

Build a classic model of flocking: no leader and no plan, just each bird reacting to the neighbours it can see.

WORK

  • Built the simulation in Python (pygame) from three local rules:
    • Alignment: steer toward the average heading of neighbours.
    • Cohesion: drift toward the average position of neighbours.
    • Separation: push away from anything too close.
  • Capped how fast each boid can turn and fixed its speed, which stops the flock from collapsing into one point or flying apart.
  • Ported the same rules to JavaScript as the flock of fish behind this site, steering around text and images.

RESULTS

  • Whole flocks form, turn together and split apart from the three rules alone.
  • Runs as the live background on every page of this site.

WHAT I LEARNED

  • Boids was my first personal project in Python after completing the course I was following.
  • The major challenges were making the code efficient and getting the interactions between boids to work properly, as it was hard to tell whether it was working.
