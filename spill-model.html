Simulating how a contaminant spill spreads through groundwater

personal · python, numpy, matplotlib · written without AI

OBJECTIVE

Extend a simple random walk into a model of a contaminant moving through groundwater: spreading out (dispersion) while the flow carries it downstream (advection).

WORK

  • Built a particle model in Python where each particle drifts downstream with the flow and takes a random jump every time step.
  • Ran the model two ways: an instantaneous spill of 10,000 particles, and a continuous leak releasing a new batch every step.
  • Added an optional decay rate for contaminants that break down over time.

RESULTS

  • Instantaneous spill: the puff drifts downstream and grows wider and flatter, so its peak concentration keeps falling.
  • Continuous leak: the plume stretches out behind a moving front and settles into a near-constant concentration near the source.

WHAT I LEARNED

  • The most difficult part was understanding which assumptions I could make to simplify the original paper's simulation.
